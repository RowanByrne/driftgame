## Gemini CLI only

- **Workers** run as their own sessions, one per lane, each started in its lane's folder with `docs/workflow/WORKER.md` (MODELS.md "Workers by agent").
- **Studio tools** come from the Roblox Studio MCP, registered as `Roblox_Studio` in `~/.gemini/settings.json` (WORKSTATION.md).
