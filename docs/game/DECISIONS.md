# Decisions

The user's decisions about this game, so they aren't reopened later. Before proposing something, check it hasn't already been decided here.

- **One line each, newest at the bottom:** the date, the decision, and why. Quote the user's own words when they say it best.
- **Phase gates go here too:** when the user says go at a gate (docs/workflow/WORKFLOW.md).
- **Never delete a line.** When a decision changes, add a new line and mark the old one "superseded by <date>".
- **Detail lives elsewhere.** A decision that changes a design or contract also updates that doc (`GDD.md`, `CONTRACTS.md`, a feature spec); this line just records that it was decided and why.

| 2026-11-04 | The tuner's three simple stats — Acceleration, Handling, Top speed, 0..10 — **drive** the eight detailed numbers rather than describing them, and 5 on every stat is the approved car. The axis is absolute, which is what lets the four archetypes land on four different Handling numbers; a car tuned off the curve reads `custom`. | 2026-11-04 | The endless road is **50 studs wide**, not the 35 first built. Barriers unchanged | The user drove the 35 and said so: "make it 50 studs wide. barriers are fine." It is one number, `Config.Road.Width`, but not a free one: a piece's footprint is half the width plus the barrier, so at 50 every piece takes 15 studs more room and the road crowds itself sooner — at 50 the pre-`T-052` generator boxed itself in on all five test seeds where at 35 it ran 500 pieces. The piece radii, arcs and gates were each measured at 35 and were deliberately left alone (`features/track-generation.md`) |
| 2026-11-04 | Impossible Road and Subway Surfers join DRIFTO as the games the endless mode borrows from | The user's call: "add 'impossible road' to the docs as an inspired by game. and subway surfers. we will be working to take elements from both for the endless mode." Impossible Road is the shape of the road itself; Subway Surfers is the framing of a run and the cosmetic meta that pays for it. What each lends, and what we do differently, is in `PITCH.md` "Games we borrow from". Neither settles what a run IS -- that stays open (`features/endless-run.md`) |
| 2026-11-04 | The game's first two sounds: the engine loop is Creator Store `5015124019` ("Car idle") and the tyre squeal is `124978127809544` ("tyre_slide"). **There is no backfire.** The sound direction is set with them: weighty, clean, never harsh; realistic rather than cartoon; the squeal on top and the engine under it | The user picked them by ear from nine candidates played on the card, having first told us the rule: "when doing sound work u are supposed to make them 1 click playable for me to test". The backfire was dropped because its only non-manufacturer-named candidate ran 0.9 s against a pop that wants under 0.5, and several in a row would smear. Both picks are used by id with nothing uploaded, so there is no master and no manifest (`AUDIO.md`, `features/car-fx.md`). Most of the Creator Store's car audio was unusable: unlabelled re-uploads, or named after real manufacturers |
| 2026-11-04 | Gravity acts on the car while it is on the ground, along the surface, as the reference does it. One number, `Config.Drift.GravityOnGround`; 0 gives the old model back exactly. **The first change to the drift model since the feel was approved** | The user asked for a downhill road and said what it should feel like: "when i slide over a crest and into a downhill drift, im sliding faster and not slowing down as much as i would if it was flat". The decompile answered it: the reference never turns gravity off, and four suspension springs cancel only the part pointing into the surface, so the car runs away downhill with no code that mentions slopes. Ours is kinematic and needs the spring's result rather than the spring. Measured: on a 9.3 degree slope, 4 seconds from 40 studs/s, the car gains 20.5 studs/s downhill and loses 21.0 uphill, where before it did neither. On the flat the term is exactly zero and every suite reproduces its old numbers step for step (`features/road-elevation.md`, `features/drift-model.md`) |
| 2026-11-06 | The touge's banked corner ships at **10 degrees of bank**, not the 30 the spec asks for. The camera keeps its shipped distance (46 studs back at 22 degrees) and rolls **into** the corner | The user chose all three by driving. On the bank: "try 10 and live with it", knowing the cost — the test autopilot spends 40 seconds stuck in a 50-piece drive at 10 against half a second at 5, because with gravity along the ground (`T-065`) the car slides down the bank into the barrier and cannot get off it. **Nothing that stops it is road:** it still reaches all 50 pieces, never leaves the road and is never airborne, so the drive check now asserts those three at zero and gives the stuck time a 60-second budget. The real fix is a car that can unstick itself from a wall (`T-074`); with that the bank goes to 30 (`features/road-elevation.md`) |
| 2026-11-08 | The tyre sound is **on all the time while the car is grounded and moving**, idling quietly and swelling into the squeal as the slip opens, the way the reference does it, and its peak drops to about half of what it was. **The clip itself stays the 3-second `124978127809544`** — three longer Creator Store candidates were played and the user kept the one in the game | The user said the squeal "isn't great. I can hear the sound looping and it's ear grating", and asked how the reference drives its own. Its code answered both halves: its squeal never stops while grounded and above its minimum speed, so its loop never restarts in the player's ear, and its volume curve reaches only about half at a full 90-degree drift where ours reached all of it. On the always-on question: "i guess the way drifto does it makes sense - always on." Then, given 6.8 s, 6.4 s and 10.9 s alternatives against the current 3.0 s, they picked "Keep the one we have", so the seam is answered by never restarting the clip rather than by a longer one. **The reference's own audio was never heard and never will ship:** its decompile is source only, with no audio file in it, and an upload that matched would be fingerprinted (`AUDIO.md`, `features/car-fx.md`, `PLATFORM-RISKS.md`) |
| 2026-11-08 | The always-on tyre hiss is **rejected after one drive**: the squeal is silent again unless a tyre is actually sliding. Its volume is now the **lateral slide speed** (`speed × sin(slip)`), not the slip angle, and its peak drops from 0.468 to 0.340 through the master | The user drove the always-on version the same afternoon they approved it: *"much better, but still could be better. the permanent squeel doesn't work for me. do it like drifto, but it should be inaudible when you start driving. if no tires are drifting, no squeal should be heard. It's to loud still generally. it's volume should be based off how hard it's actually sliding and/or drifting."* The third sentence is the real change and it is **not** what the reference does — the reference drives the squeal off the angle alone, so a car pivoting at walking pace screams as loudly as one flat out. Slide speed is both "how fast" and "how sideways" at once, which is what a real tyre's noise follows. Measured: 15° at 40 studs/s drops about 8 dB and a 90° pivot at 10 studs/s about 13.6 dB, while a genuine fast slide is only 2.8 dB down. `RollVolume` is kept at 0 so the always-on version is one number away. **The clip still never stops** — silence is a volume, not a stop, so its loop seam is still never re-heard (`AUDIO.md`, `features/car-fx.md`) |
