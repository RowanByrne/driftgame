## The look

<!-- answer: look -->
### How should it look?

stylized low poly.  I think i want cars to be notice-ably low poly.  but if the game dev goes great, i will bring in an artist to design the cars most likely.  The environment can be very very low poly, but it's important that driving surfaces are simple and easy to drift on, so a low poly road with notice-able segmented flat sections that make up a larger elevation change wouldn't work.  ramps and berms would need to be high enough fidelity that the drift mechanics can function on them well.
